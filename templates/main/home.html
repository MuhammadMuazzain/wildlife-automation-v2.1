Community garden plot registry

PlotDesk helps neighbourhood garden networks replace ad-hoc forms and shared spreadsheets with a single place to collect plot applications, review grow sites, and hand plots to coordinators.

Growers apply online. Coordinators can search, assign, and view active plots on a map — without retyping email summaries into Excel.
